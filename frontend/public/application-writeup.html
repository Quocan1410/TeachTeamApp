eduTeam writeup

Live app: https://teach-team-app.vercel.app/

eduTeam is a university hiring app for tutors and lab assistants. Candidates browse courses, filter roles, and apply with skills plus a short motivation. Lecturers shortlist, rank, or decline—and can message candidates per application.

I owned the product decisions end-to-end: Next.js UI, Express + TypeORM + MySQL API, WebAuthn passkeys, Socket.IO notifications, avatar crop, and a separate admin app so ops stays out of hiring UX.

Why: campus hiring is usually email and spreadsheets. I wanted one focused workflow where applying and reviewing feel real, and roles cannot blur.

My calls / tradeoffs: email domain picks candidate vs lecturer; one passkey per account; debounced search and paginated course cards; chat stays open (no inactivity lock). Email/authenticator password recovery stayed stubs so passkey + current-password change stayed solid first. Admin is another repo to keep this app focused.
